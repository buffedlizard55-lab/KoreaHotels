# Busan — Expanded Hotels & Booking Guide

**Planned stay:** 8 nights (Nov 14 – Nov 22)
**Coverage:** **20 hotels** across Haeundae/Dongbaek, Seomyeon/BIFC, Busan Station/Jungang, and Songdo.

## Split-stay strategy

Busan is geographically large. For eight nights, the most useful split remains:

1. **Haeundae / Dongbaek / Mipo** for the beach, coastal train, BEXCO, Gwangalli access, and resort facilities.
2. **Busan Station / Jungang / Nampo** for KTX, Jagalchi, BIFF Square, Gamcheon, and old downtown.

**Seomyeon/BIFC** is the one-hotel compromise because Lines 1 and 2 meet there. **Songdo** is a quieter beach choice but requires bus or taxi rather than a walkable metro.

## Best fits by use

- **Best Haeundae value with queen:** Shilla Stay Deluxe.
- **Best Haeundae king-room value:** Fairfield by Marriott Busan.
- **Best beach luxury value:** Paradise Hotel or The Westin Josun.
- **Best modern beach splurge:** SIGNIEL BUSAN.
- **Best laundry-equipped Haeundae stay:** ibis Ambassador or L7 HAEUNDAE.
- **Best KTX convenience:** ASTI, Ramada Encore Station, or Toyoko Inn Station No.1.
- **Best Seomyeon one-hotel compromise:** Avani for value; LOTTE HOTEL BUSAN for full-service facilities.
- **Best quieter beach:** Fairfield Songdo for value; Wyndham Grand for luxury.

## Transport cautions

- Haeundae to Nampo is a substantial cross-city journey; do not judge Busan distances by a map alone.
- Songdo has no walkable Busan Metro station. Use buses or taxis.
- A hotel beside Busan Station is ideal on a KTX travel day but less convenient for returning from Haeundae late every night.
- Normal check-in hours do not establish 24-hour late check-in. The special 24-hour rule only applies to the ICN arrival night in Seoul.

## Booking checks

1. Match the exact bed category: several “Double” rooms do not have a published 150 cm width.
2. For Haeundae resorts, verify whether pool, spa, sauna, or lounge access is included in the selected rate.
3. Compare one eight-night booking against a Haeundae + Station/Nampo split after including transfer time.
4. Recheck 11:00 versus 12:00 checkout; it varies across this list.
