# Cheonan / Asan — Verified Alternative-City Hotel Guide

**Coverage:** 7 distinct, source-verified properties
**Role:** optional replacement for the Gyeongju leg, not part of the current Seoul → Gyeongju → Busan plan

## Important rail correction

Cheonan has two different rail hubs:

- **Cheonan Station** serves downtown and Seoul Subway Line 1.
- **Cheonan-Asan Station** is the main KTX/SRT high-speed station and is several kilometers southwest of central Cheonan.

None of the seven audited properties is currently marked as a complete green core-needs match. Earlier notes overstated station walkability or assumed queen widths that the identity sources did not establish. The planner now takes the conservative approach: verify the exact bed and use a taxi/local transit where noted.

## District choice

- **Cheonan Station / downtown:** Brown Dot Dongnam is the closest audited entry, at roughly a 12-minute walk.
- **Buldang / business district:** ON City is modern and practical, but it is not beside KTX.
- **KTX/SRT trips:** no listed hotel is truly station-adjacent; budget for taxis.
- **Resort stay:** SONO Belle is a water-park/family resort, not a convenient city or KTX hotel.

## Booking checks

1. Do not treat “Double” as a confirmed 150 cm queen bed.
2. Search the exact canonical name and street address; Cheonan has several Brown Dot branches.
3. Use **Cheonan-Asan** when searching KTX and **Cheonan** for downtown Line 1 service.
4. These are later-trip alternatives, so they are not part of the special ICN arrival-night shortlist.
