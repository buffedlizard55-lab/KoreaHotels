## Important rail correction

Cheonan has two different rail hubs:

- **Cheonan Station** serves downtown and Seoul Subway Line 1.
- **Cheonan-Asan Station** is the main KTX/SRT high-speed station and is several kilometers southwest of central Cheonan.

None of the seven audited properties is a complete green core-needs match. The planner stays conservative: verify the exact bed and use a taxi/local transit where noted.

## District choice

- **Cheonan Station / downtown:** Brown Dot Dongnam is the closest audited entry (~12 min walk) but has **no dated Booking quote**.
- **Buldang / business district:** ON City is modern but sold out for this window and is not beside KTX.
- **KTX/SRT trips:** no listed hotel is truly station-adjacent; budget for taxis.
- **Resort stay:** SONO Belle is a water-park/family resort, not a convenient city or KTX hotel.

## Booking checks

1. Do not treat “Double” as a confirmed 150 cm queen bed.
2. Search the exact canonical name and street address. **Brown Dot Dongnam ≠ Brown Dot Buldang.**
3. Use **Cheonan-Asan** when searching KTX and **Cheonan** for downtown Line 1.
4. Best Western Asan listing title is now **“Best Western Asan Hotel”**; the property is in Asan. Inside 3 days the cancel fee is the **total stay**.
5. These are later-trip alternatives, so they are not part of the ICN arrival-night shortlist.
