## The transport caveat

Gyeongju has no subway, and **Singyeongju KTX Station is outside the normal accommodation districts**. None of these entries is a full core-needs match because a practical walk from KTX is not available. “Good transport” here means:

- walkable to Old Town sights and local buses;
- beside Gyeongju Intercity Bus Terminal; or
- inside the Bomun resort bus/taxi network.

## Choose the district first

- **Old Town / Hwangnidan-gil / Cheomseongdae** — walking to Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Woljeonggyo.
- **City center / intercity bus terminal** — easiest luggage transfer; short walk to Hwangnidan-gil.
- **Bomun Lake** — large hotels, pools, spas; bus/taxi for Old Town.
- **Bulguksa / Tohamsan** — quiet resort near Bulguksa and Seokguram.

## Best fits by use

- **Best conventional Old Town access:** Rivertain — beside the intercity terminal; cheapest one-bed refundable ($81) with breakfast. Adults-only; total-stay penalty inside 3 days.
- **Best western king:** Hilton Premium King $257.
- **Best western queen (named King):** Lahan Deluxe King Suite $265.
- **Best budget Bomun one-bed:** Swiss Rosen Deluxe Double $83.
- **Best for travelers with a dog:** KINOCK ($138).
- **Best for Bulguksa:** Kolon — harsh prepay; select the 1-full option.
- **Best hanok with a listed king:** HanokInn — sold out for the full window; try direct.
- **Best fully traditional location:** Wiyeonjae — all ondol; book wiyeonjae.kr.

## Late arrival at Gyeongju properties

The ICN late-arrival requirement applies only to the first night in Seoul. Still:

- Hwangnamkwan check-in **ends 22:00**.
- SONO's official room page says to contact the desk before arrival after 22:00.
- Smaller hanok stays are owner-operated and need an arranged arrival time.

Plan the Seoul-to-Gyeongju transfer during the day.

## Booking checks

1. Confirm whether “double” means a western bed and ask for width if 150 cm is essential.
2. For ondol, ask how many floor mattresses are supplied and whether the bathroom is inside the room.
3. Book Old Town and small hanok inventory early — the 6-night window is already unsellable on Booking for several properties.
4. Read total-stay penalties (Rivertain, Kolon, The-K, Swiss Rosen) before paying.
