# Room Match — two people · one queen/king bed · private bath · practical rail access

This guide mirrors the green **Core needs match** filter in the website.

## The rule

A green match must have all of the following in the planner data:

1. A **single queen or king bed** for two people, approximately 150 cm wide or larger — not two singles pushed together.
2. A **private en-suite bathroom**.
3. **Practical walkable subway/KTX access** for that city.

A room named “Double” is not automatically queen width. Where a hotel uses “Hollywood Double” or generic “Double,” follow the card's named room and reconfirm the actual bed in live inventory.

> The first Seoul night has one extra rule: a current 24-hour reception signal with an evidence link. A green core-needs badge alone is not enough. See [arrival-night.md](arrival-night.md).

## Gyeongju: bed matches exist, station match does not

Gyeongju has no subway, and Singyeongju KTX is outside Old Town and Bomun. No realistic central Gyeongju stay receives the complete green badge.

Strong western-bed/private-bath choices, if the station-distance rule is relaxed:

- **Lahan Select Gyeongju — Queen**
- **Hilton Gyeongju — King or Deluxe Queen**
- **Nadul Hanok — room with a bed and private bathroom**, but ask for bed width
- **Rivertain — conventional double room by the intercity terminal**, but ask for bed width

For HanokInn and Wiyeonjae, confirm ondol/futon setup rather than assuming a western mattress.

Verify on the official booking screen:

- exact room name and bed size/configuration;
- two-adult occupancy and private bathroom;
- live total, taxes, breakfast, and cancellation deadline;
- the local transport trade-off;
- written late-arrival confirmation for the first Seoul night.
