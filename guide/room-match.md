# Room Match — two people · one queen/king bed · private bath · practical rail access

This guide mirrors the green **“Core needs match”** filter in the website.

## The rule

A green match must have all of the following in the recorded planner data:

1. A **single queen or king bed** for two people (approximately 150 cm wide or larger), not two singles or a 140 cm double.
2. A **private en-suite bathroom**.
3. **Practical walkable subway/KTX access** for that city.

A single *double* bed can still be one bed, but it is **not** automatically a match under the queen-or-larger preference. The normal hotel cards now state why a property needs a check rather than applying a misleading green badge.

> The first night in Seoul has one extra rule: a verified 24-hour reception signal. See [arrival-night.md](arrival-night.md).

## ⚠️ Gyeongju: bed matches exist, station match does not

Gyeongju has no subway, and **Singyeongju KTX station is outside** the Old Town/Bomun hotel districts. No realistic central Gyeongju option therefore gets the complete green badge.

If you relax only the station-distance rule, the strongest one-bed/private-bath choices are:

| Hotel | Room to choose | Typical trip to KTX |
|---|---|---|
| **Lahan Select Gyeongju** | Queen | Taxi/bus to Singyeongju (~20 min) |
| **Hilton Gyeongju** | King, Deluxe Queen, or Executive King | Taxi/bus to Singyeongju (~20 min) |

For a hanok stay, confirm whether the reserved room uses a western mattress or ondol bedding before booking.

## Booking check before paying

The planner is an organizing aid, not a live guarantee. On the official booking screen, verify:

- the exact room name and bed configuration
- the number of adults and guests
- the private bathroom shown in the room description
- the cancellation terms and total, not just the nightly headline rate
- a late-arrival acknowledgement for the **first Seoul night**
