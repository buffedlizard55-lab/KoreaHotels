# Suwon — Verified Alternative-City Hotel Guide

**Coverage:** 5 distinct, source-verified properties  
**Role:** practical alternative or side-trip base near Seoul. Suwon Station is on the main Gyeongbu KTX high-speed corridor and Seoul Metro Line 1 (~30–40 min from central Seoul). The main attraction is **Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (UNESCO)**.

**Live rates last re-read:** 2026-08-18T19:16Z (Nov 1–9, 2026, 8 nights, 2 adults). See [verification-suwon-gyeongju-cheonan-daejeon-2026-08-18.md](verification-suwon-gyeongju-cheonan-daejeon-2026-08-18.md).

## The rail & location cautions

- **Suwon Station** is the primary high-speed rail and metro hub.
- **Suwon Hwaseong Fortress** is in central Suwon, about 15–20 minutes by bus/taxi from Suwon Station.
- **Suwon City Hall Station** (Suin-Bundang Line) serves Ingye-dong / City Hall. That is **not** the KTX station.
- **Gwanggyo New Town** (Courtyard) is about 30 minutes from Suwon Station and Hwaseong via local transit.

Only **Novotel Ambassador Suwon** is marked as a green core-needs match because of its **direct Suwon Station (KTX + subway)** connection and confirmed king bedding.

## Best fits by use

- **Best overall convenience:** Novotel Ambassador Suwon (direct covered access to Suwon Station KTX/metro). Book Accor — Booking is sold out for these dates.
- **Best modern business comfort:** Four Points (opened 2022, Ingye-dong). Book **Premier King**, not Premier Double.
- **Best quiet scenic stay:** Courtyard (Gwanggyo Lake Park).
- **Best budget brand value:** ibis Ambassador Suwon (renovated 2021, 2 min from City Hall subway).
