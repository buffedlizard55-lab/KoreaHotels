**Capture window:** 2026-08-21, ~18:30 UTC

## Decision rule used on every line

A row was written into the master list only when the fetched, dated Booking page itself showed all of:

1. the **room name**,
2. the **bed count and Booking's bed label** for that exact row,
3. a **price** (per night and/or 6-night total) with its tax line,
4. the **cancellation sentence verbatim**, and
5. the **prepayment terms**.

Anything missing is written as missing. A Booking "queen"/"king" label proves the OTA's bed *category*, **not** a millimetre width — where an official page has not published a dimension, this report says so instead of inferring one. Bed **size** evidence stays sourced from the official brand page already stored on each record; this pass re-verified **refundable status, current price, bed count and the bed label on the sold row**.

> These are 6-night Nov 9–15 figures. **Do not compare them with the Nov 1–9 (8-night) or Nov 15–22 (7-night) tables.** Each window is a separate search.

## Irregularities flagged for review

1. **"King" rooms sold with a full/queen bed label.** Three Busan properties name a room *King* while Booking states a different bed for the row actually sold: **The Westin Josun Busan** (Deluxe Park King → "1 full bed"), **Avani Central Busan** (Deluxe King → "1 queen bed"), and **L7 Haeundae** in the other direction (Superior **Queen** Town View → "1 full bed"). The bed on the contract is the Booking label, not the marketing name. Confirm in writing before paying.
2. **Fairfield Songdo's one-king row is unconfirmed for this window.** The $127/$761 refundable price is real and dated, but the captured chunk only *named* the 2-twin sea-view room at that price. Treat Fairfield Songdo as **price known / bed unknown** for Nov 9–15.
3. **Three "refundable" rows are not pay-at-property.** ASTI (Partner Offer, pay in advance, **no modifications**), Wyndham Grand (pay the property before arrival) and the cheaper Shilla Stay row (Partner Offer, pay in advance) all show "free cancellation" *and* a prepayment obligation. Those are different risks; re-read them at checkout.
4. **Four properties penalise the TOTAL stay, not one night.** Toyoko Inn Haeundae 2, Toyoko Inn Busan Station No.1 and Wyndham Grand charge the **whole reservation** inside 1 day; Crown Harbor charges the whole reservation inside 2 days; Avani's **no-show** fee is the whole reservation. On a 6-night Busan stay that is a materially bigger exposure than a first-night fee.
5. **Avani's headline price is a members-only price.** The $100/night figure Booking shows first is **non-refundable**, and the refundable $108/night is a Genius + pay-online rate. The public refundable price is **$132/night ($790)**. Do not budget the headline number.
6. **Scarcity on the good rooms.** Avani Deluxe King: **1 left**. Crown Harbor Executive Double City: **2 left**. Paradise: **2 left and only two rate rows offered at all** for Nov 9–15. Shilla Stay, L7 and Fairfield Haeundae: 5 left.
7. **Two ibis Haeundae records cannot be priced at all** until a Booking (or other dated) property URL is added to `data/hotels.json`. Their existing Nov 15–22 "rates" have no UTC timestamp and are therefore **not** live quotes.
8. **Window-labelling bug found and fixed.** `findings.py` was labelling every `refundableRate` row as "Nov 1–9" regardless of its actual stay dates, so all 20 Busan (Nov 15–22) and 15 Gyeongju (Nov 9–15) captures were being displayed under the wrong date heading. The window is now derived from each row's own `stayCheckIn` / `stayCheckOut`.
9. **Itinerary conflict, unresolved on purpose.** `data/itinerary.json` and `hotels.json → split` still describe **Gyeongju** Nov 9–15 and **Busan** Nov 15–22. This request treats **Busan** as the Nov 9–15 stay. Both sets of captures are kept; the itinerary text was **not** rewritten, because changing the planned route is a decision, not a verification. Say the word and it will be updated.
10. **Partner-offer room names lost at page-chunk boundaries.** For ASTI and Paradise, a second, cheaper refundable row exists whose room label was split across the fetch boundary. The price is recorded in the note but the room name is deliberately left blank rather than guessed.
