## 3. Flagged Irregularities (Gyeongju & Busan)

1. **Hwangnamkwan Check-in Limitation:** The hanok village has a strict check-in cutoff of **22:00**. There is no 24-hour reception desk. Late arrivals must proceed to conventional city hotels.
2. **SONO Calm renamed:** Formerly known as *Daemyung Resort Gyeongju*, it is renamed to **SONO Calm Gyeongju** but legacy OTA listings still use the old slug `daemyung-resort-gyeongju`.
3. **Ramada Plaza Suwon Deluxe King:** Booking.com and Wyndham inventories explicitly show "Deluxe King" as possessing a **1 queen bed** (150 cm), which is a common naming mismatch to watch out for.
4. **Haeundae Ramada Adults Only:** Several major booking directories list `busan-ramada-encore-haeundae` as an "Adults-Only" property, which represents a crucial constraint if traveling with family.
5. **No Subway in Gyeongju / Songdo Beach:** The entire Gyeongju leg and beachfront hotels in Songdo (Wyndham Grand, Fairfield Songdo) have **no walkable rail/metro access**. Bus or taxi transfers are required.
