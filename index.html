      How this shortlist is meant to be used

      What the green “core needs” mark means

      It flags a planner entry with a one-bed room for two, a private bathroom, and walkable rail/subway access. Select the named room type when booking: a hotel can also sell twin rooms or smaller double beds that do not meet the preference.

      Gyeongju is the deliberate exception: its KTX station is outside the usual Old Town and Bomun hotel areas, so no practical city hotel is a walkable rail stay.

      How the arrival-night evidence works

      • Use each card’s evidence link to re-check the 24-hour-reception claim before paying.
      • Use the official booking link for the reservation itself where possible.
      • See the detailed arrival-night research note in this repo for source links and the booking-message template.

      Identity and duplicate audit

      All 84 records have a sourced canonical property name and a unique identity link. Validation rejects duplicate IDs, canonical names, official URLs, verification URLs, and exact coordinates; highly similar branch names require an explicit distinct-property cross-check.
