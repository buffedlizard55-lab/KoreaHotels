      What the green “core needs” mark means

      It flags a planner entry with a one-bed room for two, a private bathroom, and walkable rail/subway access. Select the named room type when booking: a hotel can also sell twin rooms or smaller double beds that do not meet the preference.

      Gyeongju is the deliberate exception: its KTX station is outside the usual Old Town and Bomun hotel areas, so no practical city hotel is a walkable rail stay.

      How the arrival-night evidence works
